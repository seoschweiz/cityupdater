Chicago Real Estate

Chicago has a large and diverse real estate market that includes downtown apartments, historic residential neighborhoods, luxury properties, suburban homes, commercial offices, retail spaces and industrial properties.

Housing conditions can vary considerably depending on location, transportation access, property type and neighborhood demand. This makes Chicago real estate a constantly changing market for buyers, renters, investors and property professionals.

Chicago Housing Market

Residential property in Chicago ranges from high-rise condominiums and rental apartments to townhouses and detached homes. Different neighborhoods offer different combinations of housing density, architecture, amenities and access to public transportation.

Chicago Apartments and Rentals

Renting is an important part of the Chicago housing market. Apartments are available across a wide range of neighborhoods, from central areas close to downtown employment to residential districts farther from the city center.

Commercial Real Estate in Chicago

Chicago is also a major commercial real estate market. Office towers, retail properties, warehouses, hotels and mixed-use developments form an important part of the city's economy.

Neighborhood Development

Real estate development often plays an important role in the evolution of Chicago neighborhoods. New residential buildings, public spaces, restaurants, shops and transportation improvements can attract additional residents and investment.
