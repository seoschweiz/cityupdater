Chicago Jobs

Chicago has one of the largest metropolitan labor markets in the United States. The city supports employment across finance, healthcare, technology, transportation, hospitality, education, manufacturing, professional services, retail and many other industries.

Its central location and extensive transportation infrastructure have helped Chicago develop into an important business center. Large corporations, startups, universities, hospitals, government institutions and small businesses all contribute to the local employment market.

Chicago Business and Professional Jobs

Downtown Chicago and surrounding business districts support many positions in finance, law, consulting, marketing, technology, insurance and corporate services.

Healthcare Jobs in Chicago

Healthcare is another major source of employment. Hospitals, clinics, research institutions and healthcare companies create opportunities for doctors, nurses, technicians, administrators, researchers and support staff.

Hospitality and Tourism Employment

Chicago's tourism and convention industries support a large number of jobs. Hotels, restaurants, event venues, attractions and entertainment businesses employ workers in hospitality, food service, management, sales and customer service.

Transportation and Logistics Jobs

Chicago is one of North America's most important transportation and logistics hubs. Airports, rail systems, freight networks, warehouses and distribution centers create employment across transportation, logistics and supply chain operations.

Technology and Startup Jobs

Chicago has also developed a growing technology and startup ecosystem. Opportunities can include software development, data analysis, cybersecurity, digital marketing, product management and other technology-related roles.
