Chicago Restaurants

Chicago has one of the most diverse restaurant scenes in the United States. The city is known for classic local dishes as well as modern fine dining, neighborhood restaurants, international cuisine, bakeries, cafés, bars and innovative food concepts.

Dining in Chicago reflects the city's diverse population and long history of immigration. Visitors and residents can find restaurants representing cuisines from around the world alongside famous Chicago specialties and contemporary American cooking.

Chicago Food Culture

Chicago food culture extends far beyond its famous deep-dish pizza. Italian beef sandwiches, Chicago-style hot dogs and other local specialties remain important parts of the city's culinary identity, but the restaurant scene is constantly evolving.

Neighborhood Restaurants in Chicago

Different Chicago neighborhoods offer very different dining experiences. Areas close to downtown contain many restaurants serving visitors, office workers and residents, while neighborhood districts often have independent establishments with strong local followings.

Chicago Fine Dining

Chicago also has an established fine-dining scene. The city attracts chefs, hospitality professionals and food enthusiasts from across the country and internationally. High-end restaurants, tasting menus and chef-driven concepts contribute to Chicago's reputation as a major culinary destination.

Restaurant Openings and Food News

Restaurant news in Chicago can include new openings, chef appointments, closings, menu changes, restaurant awards, food festivals and developments in the hospitality industry.
