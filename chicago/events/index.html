Chicago Events

Chicago hosts events throughout the year, ranging from major festivals and concerts to exhibitions, neighborhood celebrations, sporting events, markets, conferences and community activities.

The city's size and cultural diversity mean there is usually something happening across different parts of Chicago. Large downtown events may attract visitors from around the world, while smaller neighborhood events provide a more local experience.

Chicago Festivals

Festivals are an important part of Chicago's event calendar. Music, food, culture, art and neighborhood celebrations can take place across the city, particularly during the warmer months.

Concerts and Live Entertainment

Chicago has a long history of live music and remains an important destination for concerts and performances. Large arenas, theaters, clubs and smaller venues host artists covering many different musical styles.

Art and Cultural Events

Museums, galleries and cultural institutions regularly organize exhibitions, talks, screenings and public programs. Chicago's arts community also supports independent galleries, artist-run spaces and neighborhood cultural events.

Business and Professional Events

Chicago is also a major destination for conferences, conventions, trade shows and professional gatherings. Its central location, transportation connections and large convention infrastructure make it an important meeting point for many industries.
