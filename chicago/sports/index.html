Chicago Sports

Chicago is one of the most recognized sports cities in the United States. Professional teams, historic stadiums and passionate supporters play an important role in the city's identity. Baseball, basketball, football, hockey and soccer all have strong followings across Chicago and the surrounding region.

Sports in Chicago are closely connected with the city's neighborhoods and traditions. Major games can influence everything from local traffic and restaurant activity to tourism and entertainment.

Chicago Professional Sports Teams

Chicago is home to several major professional sports organizations. Baseball fans follow both the Chicago Cubs and Chicago White Sox. The Chicago Bears represent the city in professional football, while the Chicago Bulls have built an internationally recognized basketball brand. Hockey fans follow the Chicago Blackhawks.

Baseball in Chicago

Baseball is deeply rooted in Chicago sports culture. The Chicago Cubs play at Wrigley Field, one of the best-known baseball stadiums in the United States. On the South Side, the Chicago White Sox have their own long-established following.

Chicago Basketball and Hockey

The United Center is a major destination for Chicago sports fans. It is home to both the Chicago Bulls and Chicago Blackhawks and hosts a wide range of sporting and entertainment events throughout the year.

Chicago Football

Football is another major part of the Chicago sports landscape. The Chicago Bears have a large and loyal following, and discussion around the team extends far beyond game days.

Sports Events in Chicago

Chicago also hosts running events, college sports, amateur competitions, youth sports and major sporting occasions throughout the year. The Chicago Marathon is one of the city's best-known international sporting events.
